# Step 1: Orientation

covers the target (initiative-, epic-, story-, spike- or bug-*.md
under the initiative folder, or an older SPEC*.md), read it first
and in full and trust it. Then use the plan, PR description, and
commit messages when they exist. Ask the user questions until you
know both.
